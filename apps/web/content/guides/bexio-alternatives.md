The main bexio alternatives in Switzerland are Magic Heidi, KLARA, Banana, CashCtrl, AbaNinja and Helvetly. Which one fits depends on whether you need full bookkeeping and payroll (bexio, KLARA, AbaNinja, CashCtrl, Banana) or mainly want to get paid with less admin (Magic Heidi for simple books, Helvetly for the back office around invoices).

Everything said here about other products comes from their own public websites as checked on 11 November 2026, and their prices as checked on 24 November 2026. Prices change, so check the vendor's site before you decide. Each product has a side-by-side comparison with Helvetly on its own page.

## What is bexio, and why look for an alternative?

bexio is a broad Swiss business suite with accounting, payroll and an app marketplace, made for small businesses, startups, the self-employed and their fiduciaries. People look for alternatives when they need less than a full suite, want a free plan, want a different pricing model, or need something bexio does not list on its site, such as e-invoice files for Germany or Poland.

bexio has four tiers by features and included users: Basic CHF 35, Advanced CHF 42, Optima CHF 69 and Ultimate CHF 119 a month billed yearly (CHF 45, 52, 79 and 129 billed monthly), with 1, 2, 5 and 25 users, excluding VAT. Add-ons such as payroll and bexio Pay cost extra. Expenses come with the Advanced plan, where AI also reads receipts and QR invoices.

## What is the best bexio alternative for a freelancer?

For a freelancer with simple books and few invoices, Magic Heidi is the closest fit: it is made for the Swiss self-employed and has a free plan for 3 invoices and 3 receipts. If you keep your own double-entry books, Banana and CashCtrl are low-cost options; if you want contracts, retainers and a client portal around your invoices, Helvetly fits better.

## What is the best bexio alternative for a small team?

For a small team that wants accounting, payroll and invoicing from one Swiss vendor, KLARA and AbaNinja are the closest alternatives to bexio. For a team that mainly needs to get paid and work with clients, Helvetly is built for that: everyone works in the same records at the same time, with roles for owner, admins and members in every paid plan.

- **KLARA** bundles accounting, payroll, a till and an online shop as packages and modules, with roles for admins, trustees, payroll, accounting and more. It suits shops and venues that need a till with card terminals.
- **AbaNinja** packs invoicing, accounting and payroll from the Abacus world into one app, with user groups and permissions per area. It suits teams that need payroll with ELM and salary certificates, or expect to move to Abacus ERP later.
- **CashCtrl** has roles for admins, guests and trustees, and runs payroll for a few employees alongside the books. It is a web app for computers; phone browsers are not supported.
- **Banana** has multi-user subscriptions, but one person edits a file at a time.
- **Helvetly** adds a client portal (Business), contracts sent for simple electronic signature (Pro and Business) and retainers with hour tracking.

## Which bexio alternatives have a free plan?

Magic Heidi, Banana, CashCtrl, AbaNinja and Helvetly have free plans; bexio and KLARA do not. The free plans are limited: Magic Heidi to 3 invoices and 3 receipts, Banana to 70 transactions, CashCtrl to 1 user, AbaNinja to 1 user and 1,000 documents a year, and Helvetly to 1 person, 5 clients and 3 invoices and 3 quotes a month.

If you send only a few invoices a year, a free plan is hard to beat. Once you send invoices every week or work as a team, compare what the paid plans include rather than the entry price.

## Which alternative fits if you need full bookkeeping or payroll?

For full double-entry bookkeeping, CashCtrl (with year-end closing), Banana and AbaNinja are built for it; for payroll, AbaNinja (with ELM and salary certificates), KLARA and CashCtrl. Helvetly is not full accounting software and does not calculate payroll.

Helvetly keeps a double-entry journal of your invoices and expenses and exports it for Banana, bexio, Abacus or as CSV, so your fiduciary can close the books in their own tool. On the Business plan it keeps a register of your employees and of pay worked out by a person or a payroll system.

## When should you stay with bexio?

Stay with bexio if you need full bookkeeping and annual financial statements in the same tool, Swissdec-certified payroll for employees or stock management alongside invoicing, or if your fiduciary already works in bexio. Moving away from a suite that covers all of that rarely pays off.

## What does Helvetly do differently from bexio?

In Helvetly, a service business can use the following workflows.

- E-invoice XML for Germany, Poland, Romania and Peppol: XRechnung, Peppol BIS, KSeF and e-Factura files (Business). They are exported as files; Helvetly does not deliver them through the networks.
- Contracts filled from templates and sent for simple electronic signature (Pro and Business); qualified signatures are not offered.
- A client portal where clients see their invoices and shared documents (Business).
- Retainers with the hours left, and instalment plans with a QR bill for each part.
- Heidi, an assistant built in, who answers from your own records and acts only after you confirm (Pro and Business).

What Helvetly lacks compared with bexio: annual financial statements, payroll calculation, stock management and native phone apps (Helvetly is a web app for every device).

## How do you switch from bexio?

bexio exports its data per module as Excel or PDF. Helvetly can [import records with AI](/help/import-records-with-ai) from PDFs, images, CSV, Excel, Word and ZIP files, and shows you everything for review before anything is saved (Pro and Business). Start with your clients and products, then open invoices.

Switch at the start of a month or a VAT period, so that each period's invoices and VAT return sit in one tool. Keep your old records for the legal retention period of 10 years; see [what a Swiss invoice must contain](/guides/invoice-requirements).
