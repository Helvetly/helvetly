Send an issued invoice straight from Helvetly, with the PDF attached.

1. Open the issued invoice from **Invoices**.
2. Click **Send**, or choose **Send by email** / **Send again** in the **…** menu.
3. Check **To**, which defaults to the client's billing email. Fill in **Cc**, **Subject** and **Message** if you like, then send.
4. The **Emails** tab of the invoice shows each email that went out, when and by whom.

## Good to know

- You can add up to 10 addresses in To and up to 10 in Cc.
- Prefer to send it yourself? Click **Download PDF**.
