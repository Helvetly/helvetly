Keep your workspace's name and time zone up to date.

1. Go to **Settings → Workspace**.
2. Change the **Workspace name** and/or **Time zone**.
3. Click **Save**.

## Good to know

- Only an owner can rename the workspace. Owners and admins can change the time zone.
- The time zone sets today's date, due dates and engagement periods. Issued invoices keep their dates.
