When money comes in, record it on the invoice so you always know what is still due.

1. Open the invoice and click **Record payment**. You can also find it in the row's **…** menu on the list.
2. Enter the **Amount** and **Paid on** date, or click **Mark fully paid today**.
3. Optionally choose the **Payment method** and add a note that only your team sees.
4. Click **Record payment**. The status changes to Paid once nothing is due.

## Good to know

- The amount can't be more than what is still due, and the date can't be in the future.
- To fix a mistake, open the **Payments** tab and delete the payment.
- The issued PDF and its QR code keep the original amount. After a partial payment, a client using the Business portal can see the remaining balance there. For a bank transfer, they should enter that balance in their e-banking.
