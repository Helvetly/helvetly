A few keys get you almost anywhere in Helvetly without reaching for the mouse.

1. Press `?` to see every shortcut, or click your name at the bottom left and choose **Keyboard shortcuts**.
2. ⌘K or `/` opens Quick Actions, ⌘J opens or closes Heidi, and ⌘B shows or hides the sidebar. On Windows and Linux, use Ctrl instead of ⌘.
3. `H` opens Help & feedback, and `G` then `N` goes to Notifications. `D` switches the theme: light, dark, then system. `N` starts a new record, just like the page's **New** button.

## Good to know

- Single-key shortcuts only work when you are not typing in a field and no dialog or menu is open.
