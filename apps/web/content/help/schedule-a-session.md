---
title: Schedule a client session and record attendance
description: Book a one-off or recurring session on any plan. Link it to an engagement to count the client’s attendance.
category: Engagements
order: 6
updated: 2026-11-24
---

1. With scheduling edit access, go to **Scheduling → New session**. Select the client and host, set the start and duration, and choose the engagement if this session uses it.
2. For a series, choose a repeat rule. Check the location and whether to email the client, then save. Open the session in the calendar to check its date and host.
3. Once the session has started, open it and mark the client **Attended**. For a linked engagement you also need permission to edit engagements. The session then appears as an entry there.

For calendar sync on a paid plan, open scheduling settings and connect your own Google or Microsoft calendar if the provider is available. On Free you can subscribe to your private iCal feed.
