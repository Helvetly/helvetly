Clients can ask to update their details from the portal, and you decide whether to accept.

The client portal, and with it change requests, needs **Business**.

1. When a client asks in the portal to change their details (name, billing email, phone, address, VAT or registration number), owners and admins get a notification.
2. Open the client. A banner shows the requested changes.
3. Click **Approve** to apply them, or **Reject**.

## Good to know

- Only owners and admins can decide on change requests.
- Invoices that are already issued keep the old details.
