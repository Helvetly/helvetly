Deleted something by mistake? It waits in the Trash for 30 days.

1. Go to **Documents** and click **Trash** in the left panel.
2. Select items and click **Restore** to bring them back, or **Delete forever**.
3. **Empty Trash** removes everything in it.

## Good to know

- Items are deleted for good 30 days after they were moved to the Trash.
- Business records that must be kept stay.
