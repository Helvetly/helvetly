Moving from another system? Let AI read your old files and turn them into Helvetly records.

1. Open **Clients**, **Products & services**, **Engagements** or **Invoices** and choose **Import** in the page's **…** menu. For expenses, use **Upload receipts** instead.
2. Drop in spreadsheets, PDFs, photos or Word files, then click **Read files**.
3. Review the records that were found. Edit a row, untick any you don't want and resolve any conflicts.
4. Click **Import** to create the selected records, then **Done**.
5. You can also attach files in Heidi and pick "Import as". The import drawer then opens with those files.

## Good to know

- AI must be allowed for the workspace by an owner or admin, and imports use the monthly AI allowance.
- If your old system already declared VAT for the imported invoices, close those months in **Settings → Periods** first.
