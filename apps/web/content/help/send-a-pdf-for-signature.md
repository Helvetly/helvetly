Get documents signed online, without printing or scanning.

Signature requests need **Pro** or **Business** and count towards the monthly signature requests. They are simple electronic signatures, not qualified ones.

1. Go to **Documents** and click **Create → PDF for signature…**. Or right-click a PDF → **Send for signature…**.
2. Under **Document**, pick the PDF and add a title and message.
3. Under **Signers**, add each person's name and email with **Add signer** (or pick a contact). Set the **Signing order** and **Expires after**.
4. Under **Place fields**, put each signer's fields on the pages.
5. Under **Review & send**, check everything and click **Send for signature**.
6. Follow progress under **Signatures**. Each request offers **Remind**, **Revoke** and **Download PDF**, plus an audit trail.

## Good to know

- The PDF you send is frozen, so later changes to the file don't affect it.
- You can add up to 10 signers.
