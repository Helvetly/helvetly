Add a client once and reuse their details on every invoice.

1. Go to **Clients** and click **New client**. You can also use Quick Actions → New client.
2. Choose the **Client type** (company or person) and enter the name. Only the name is required.
3. Under **Address**, fill in the street, number, ZIP, city and country. You need the address before you can issue an invoice to this client.
4. If you like, add the **VAT / UID number** and the business registration number. Under **Billing defaults**, set the document language, currency and payment terms.
5. Click **Add client**. To change it later, open the client and click **Edit**.

## Good to know

- A client without a full address shows as a draft. You can draft invoices for it but not issue them.
- In the client's **More actions** (…) menu you can deactivate the client, which hides it from pickers. Owners and admins can also delete it there.
