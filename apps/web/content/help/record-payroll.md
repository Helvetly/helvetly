---
title: Add an employee and record payroll
description: Use the Business payroll register for amounts calculated by your payroll provider. An employee record does not give anyone a login.
category: Team and workspace
order: 10
updated: 2026-11-24
---

1. The workspace owner grants separate employee and payroll editing rights in **Members**. Under **Employees → New employee**, enter the employee’s details and save.
2. Open **Payroll → New entry**. Choose the employee and pay period. Enter the gross pay, deductions, net pay and employer costs from the payroll statement, then save.
3. Review and approve the entry before recording payment. If the workspace requires a second approver, another member must approve.
