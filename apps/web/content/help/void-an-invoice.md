Voiding cancels an issued invoice so nothing is owed any more.

Voiding an issued invoice needs **Pro** or **Business**. In Basic you can still edit or delete a draft.

1. Open the issued invoice and open the **…** menu.
2. Choose **Void** and confirm with **Void invoice**.
3. A full credit note is issued against it, and nothing is owed any more.

## Good to know

- Only owners and admins can void, and only open invoices with no payments and no credit notes.
- Voiding cannot be undone.
- Drafts are not voided: use **Delete draft** instead. A draft has no number, so nothing goes missing in your numbering.
