Keep your name up to date so your team knows who did what.

1. Click your name at the bottom left and choose **Settings**, or go to **Settings → Account settings**.
2. Under **Profile**, change your **Full name** and click **Save**.

## Good to know

- Your email is your sign-in address.
- Under **Upload photo** or **Change photo**, choose an image, crop it and save. Removing your uploaded photo shows your Google picture again if you connected Google.
