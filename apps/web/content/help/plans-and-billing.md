Each workspace has its own plan. A workspace without a paid plan is on **Free**. Here is how to choose and manage a plan.

1. Go to **Settings → Billing** (Plan & billing) to see your plan, its status and your usage against the limits.
2. Under **Plans**, choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**, then pick **Basic**, **Pro** or **Business**. **Compare plans** shows what each plan includes and its allowances side by side. Prices include Swiss VAT.
3. To enter a discount, click **Have a promotion code?**.
4. To update your card, cancel or get invoices, click **Manage billing**. Past invoices are listed under **Invoices**.

## Good to know

- **Free** costs nothing, needs no card and never expires; it is for one person. **Basic** includes 1 person, **Pro** 2 and **Business** 5. Each further person costs CHF 10, 15 or 20 a month. Basic allows up to 5 people, Pro up to 25, Business any number.
- **Free** and **Basic** cover invoices, quotes, Swiss QR bills, the Revolut link, engagements managed by hand, expenses and scheduling, with smaller allowances, and add "Sent with Helvetly" to client emails and PDFs. **Pro** adds Heidi, AI import, automations and payment reminders, instalments, signatures, templates and contracts, corrections, VAT returns, the ledger, data exports and the API, without that line. **Business** adds the client portal (also on your own domain), e-invoice XML, employees and a payroll register, and document approvals.
- Clients, products, engagements and scheduled sessions are fixed caps for the workspace; **Business** has no cap on them, nor on invoices, quotes, expenses and signature requests. The other allowances are per person and add up into one shared pool; a plan always counts at least the people it includes. Monthly allowances start again at 00:00 UTC on the first of the month.
- When an allowance is used up, only the new action that needs it stops; nothing is deleted and nothing is charged extra.
- New eligible trials give Pro for 14 days with up to 2 people. A card is required. During the trial you get Pro's allowances for one person, and they do not reset. Trials already granted under earlier offers keep their original terms.
- Without a paid plan, for example after the trial or a cancellation, the workspace is on Free: nothing is deleted and everything stays readable, but new additions beyond Free's limits wait until you choose a plan.
- Only owners and admins can change the plan.
- When you reach a limit, you see a prompt with **Compare plans**.
