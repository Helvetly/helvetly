Each workspace has its own plan. Here is how to choose and manage it.

1. Go to **Settings → Billing** (Plan & billing) to see your plan, its status and your usage against the limits.
2. Under **Plans**, choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**, then pick **Basic**, **Pro** or **Business**. **Compare plans** shows every limit side by side. Prices include Swiss VAT.
3. To enter a discount, click **Have a promotion code?**.
4. To update your card, cancel or get invoices, click **Manage billing**. Past invoices are listed under **Invoices**.

## Good to know

- A new workspace can start a free Basic trial. A card is required.
- There is no free plan. Without an active plan the workspace is read-only: nothing is deleted, and it opens again once a plan is chosen.
- Only owners and admins can change the plan.
- When you reach a limit, you see a prompt with **See plans**.
