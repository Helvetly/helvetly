---
title: Send a quote and turn it into an invoice
description: Prepare a quote on any plan, record the client’s decision and invoice the agreed work.
category: Invoices and getting paid
order: 15
updated: 2026-11-24
---

1. With permission to edit quotes, open **Quotes → New quote**. Choose the client, enter the work and prices, and set the validity date. Select **Save draft**.
2. Open the draft and select **Send**. If an approval policy applies, submit the draft and wait for approval first. The sent revision keeps its PDF.
3. When the client agrees, choose **Accept**. With invoice access, select **Create invoice** to open the new draft. Review it before issuing.
