Bring colleagues or your accountant into your workspace with just the access they need.

Free is for one person, so inviting someone needs a paid plan. Basic includes 1 person, Pro 2 and Business 5; each accepted member beyond those adds to the monthly price. Open invitations, clients in the portal and people asked to sign are not counted.

1. Go to **Members** and click **Invite member**.
2. Enter their **Email**.
3. Under **How to invite**, choose **By email** or **By link** (you share the link yourself).
4. Pick a **Role**: Owner, Admin or Member. For a Member, set their **Access**: Full access, Read only, Accountant or Custom per area.
5. Click **Send invitation** or **Create link**. Open invitations are on the **Invited** tab, where you can resend or revoke them.

## Good to know

- Only owners and admins can invite.
- A link works for 7 days, and only for someone signed in with that email address.
