If a client paid too much, record the refund once you have paid them back.

1. When a client paid more than they owe, for example after a credit note, the invoice shows a refund due.
2. Open the invoice and click **Record refund**.
3. Enter the **Amount**, the **Refunded on** date and, if you like, the **Refund method** and a note.
4. Click **Record refund**.

## Good to know

- Use the **Refund due** filter on **Invoices** to find invoices that still need a refund.
