The client portal gives your client a private place to find their invoices and documents.

The client portal is part of **Business**. Clients in the portal are not paid team seats.

1. Open the client. On the **Overview** tab, turn on the **Client portal** switch.
2. The client signs in with its email, or its billing email when it has no email, using a code sent to that address.
3. Turning the portal on emails the client an invitation. The **Invitation** row shows **Not sent**, **Sent** with the date or **Signed in** with the date. **Send invitation** or **Resend** emails it again.
4. The **Portal** row has the portal's address with buttons to copy or open it. In the portal, the client sees issued invoices (PDF), engagements, documents you shared and their own details.

## Good to know

- Contacts are not portal logins. The portal uses the client's own email address.
- Turning the switch off, or changing the client's email, signs the client out of the portal right away.
