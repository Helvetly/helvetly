Stuck, found a bug or have an idea? Here is how to reach us.

1. Click **Help & feedback** at the bottom of the sidebar, or choose it in your name menu or in Quick Actions.
2. For a how-to question, click **Ask Heidi** at the top and Heidi's panel opens.
3. To tell the team something, choose **Report a problem** or **Suggest an idea**.
4. Write a **Description**. You can add **Screenshots** too (drop, paste or browse): up to 5 PNG, JPEG or WebP images, 5 MB each.
5. Click **Send feedback**.

## Good to know

- Your account, workspace and the page you are on are included with your message, but your screen is not recorded.
- Remove any private information from screenshots before you send them.
