Add a Swiss QR bill so your clients can pay with a quick scan.

1. Go to **Settings → Invoices** (owners and admins).
2. Under **Bank account**, enter your **IBAN**. You need a CH or LI IBAN for a QR bill.
3. Under **Payment methods**, turn on **Swiss QR bill**.
4. New CHF and EUR invoices then get a payment slip on its own page. In the invoice editor, the **Payment** note says "QR bill attached".

## Good to know

- With a QR-IBAN, CHF invoices get a QR reference your bank matches automatically.
- A QR-IBAN works for CHF only, so EUR invoices need a normal IBAN.
- Each invoice can turn payment methods on or off above the document.
