Set up your company details once, and every invoice uses them.

1. Go to **Settings → Invoices**, or choose **Invoice settings** in the **Invoices** page menu.
2. Fill in **Logo**, **Company** (name and address) and **VAT**. Under VAT, turn on **Registered for VAT**, then add your UID with MWST, the default rate and whether you enter prices net or gross.
3. Under **Bank account**, enter your **IBAN** and bank. Under **Payment methods**, turn on **Swiss QR bill** and/or **Revolut**.
4. Under **Numbering**, pick a format: YYYY-NNN, YYYY/NNN, NNN/YYYY or RE-YYYY-NNN.
5. Set your **Defaults** (currency, document language, payment terms), **Instalments**, **Dunning** and the **Footer**.
6. When the checklist shows **Ready to issue invoices**, you're all set.

## Good to know

- Only owners and admins can change these settings.
- Numbers are given when an invoice is issued, without gaps, and restart every year.
- There is one number sequence, and credit notes get their own numbers.
