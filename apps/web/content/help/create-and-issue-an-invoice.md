Create a draft, check the PDF and issue it when you're ready.

1. Go to **Invoices** and click **New invoice**. You can also use Quick Actions or the **Invoice** button on a client.
2. Under **Bill to**, click **Choose a client**.
3. Check the **Issue date**, **Due date** and **Service** dates, and add a **Reference** (such as a PO number) if you need one.
4. Add lines with **Add line** (or press Enter) or **Add from catalogue**. Above the document you can set the **Currency**, **Document language** and **VAT**. You can also add a **Discount**, an introduction, a footer and an **Internal note**, which is never printed.
5. The draft saves by itself, and ⌘S saves right away. Click **Preview** (⌘P) to see the PDF.
6. Click **Issue invoice** and confirm. The invoice gets the next number and its PDF is stored.

## Good to know

- Issuing cannot be undone. An issued invoice can't be edited, so fixes go through a credit note or a correction.
- If anything is missing, Helvetly lists what to fix with a link, such as **Open invoice settings** or **Complete the client**.
