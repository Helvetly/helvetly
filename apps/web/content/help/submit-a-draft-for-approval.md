---
title: Submit a draft for approval
description: On Business, an owner or admin can require approval before an invoice is issued, a quote is sent or an expense is booked.
category: Team and workspace
order: 9
updated: 2026-11-24
---

1. Open **Settings → Approvals**, choose the document type and set its rules. For a group rule, add eligible members to the group first.
2. The person editing the document opens its details and chooses **Submit for approval**. An eligible reviewer opens the request and chooses **Approve** or rejects it. Authors and the submitter cannot approve their own work.
3. Once all required approvals are recorded, return to the draft and issue, send or book it. Editing its contents requires a new approval.
