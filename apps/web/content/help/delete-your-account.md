Deleting your account removes you from Helvetly for good.

1. Go to **Settings → Account settings → Delete account**.
2. Type your email to confirm and click **Delete account**.

## Good to know

- You leave every workspace and are signed out everywhere.
- This can't be undone.
- Transfer ownership, or delete workspaces where you are the last owner, first.
