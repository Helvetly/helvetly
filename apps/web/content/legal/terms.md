## 2. Accounts, workspaces and roles

- Your data lives in a **workspace**. Whoever creates a workspace is its **owner**. The owner can

- You are responsible for whom you invite and for what they do in your workspace.

## 3. Plans, trial and prices

- There is no free plan. Each workspace needs its own subscription: **Basic**, **Pro** or
  **Business**. The current limits of each plan (members, clients, invoices, expenses, products,
  engagements, signatures, storage, AI tokens) are shown in Settings → Billing. When a limit is
  reached, you can upgrade or wait for the next month (monthly limits restart each calendar month).
- Prices are **per seat**: every person in the workspace (owner, admins and members) is one seat;
  an open invitation takes no seat until it is accepted. Per seat and month: Basic CHF 19, Pro
  CHF 49 and Business CHF 99. Paying yearly costs twelve months less 20 %, rounded up to a whole
  franc (CHF 183, 471 and 951 per seat and year).
- Prices are in **CHF and include Swiss VAT** where we charge it: the price shown is the price paid.
  Where your business is abroad and the reverse charge applies, the same amount is charged without
  VAT and you account for the tax yourself.
- When people join or leave the workspace, the number of seats follows: an added seat is charged at
  once and a freed seat credited at once to your balance with Stripe (which pays your next invoices),
  each prorated from the day of the change. Subscriptions
  taken out at an earlier flat price per workspace keep that price, and its member limit, until
  their plan or billing interval changes.
- **Trial:** the first subscription of a workspace to Basic starts with a 7-day trial. A card is
  required. Without a valid card at the end of the trial, the subscription ends. Each workspace gets
  one trial only. Pro and Business are charged at once.
- Promotion codes apply only as stated with the code.

## 4. Payment, renewal, changes and cancellation

- Payments are processed by Stripe. Helvetly never sees your full card details.
- Subscriptions are paid in advance and **renew automatically** for the same period (a month or a
  year) until cancelled. If a payment fails, Stripe retries it; the plan stays active until three days
  after the end of the paid period, then the workspace is read-only until the invoice is paid.
- **Upgrades** and moving from monthly to yearly are charged at once, prorated, and apply once that
  payment goes through. **Downgrades** and moving to monthly apply at once; the unused part is credited
  to your balance with Stripe. A smaller plan must fit what the workspace already holds.
- **Cancelling:** an owner or admin can cancel any time in Settings → Billing. The plan stays until
  the end of the paid period and is not renewed.
- **Refunds:** paid periods are not refunded, including when a workspace is deleted, unless Swiss
  law requires otherwise.
- We may change prices with at least 30 days' notice by email. A new price applies from your next
  renewal; you may cancel before it does.

## 5. Read-only workspaces and data export

- A workspace without a live subscription (trial ended, cancelled, or a payment still unpaid three days
  after the paid period ended) becomes
  **read-only**: you can still see and download everything, but cannot change anything until you
  choose a plan again.
- The owner can **download the whole workspace** (records and files, as a ZIP) at any time, with
  or without a plan, in Settings → Workspace → Export all data. Everyone can download their own
  account data as JSON.

## 6. Ending the contract and deletion

- You can end the contract by cancelling every subscription and deleting your workspaces (owner only;
  the workspace name must be typed to confirm). Deleting a workspace cancels its subscription at
  once. Export your data first: after deletion nobody can reach the workspace any more.
- A deleted workspace's records stay stored but unreachable. Keeping your business records for the
  period the law requires (in Switzerland 10 years, OR Art. 958f, GeBüV) is your obligation; export
  them before you delete. What happens to stored records after deletion is described in the
  [data processing agreement](dpa.md), section 9.
- Deleting your personal account requires handing over or deleting the workspaces you are the last
  owner of first. Your sign-in methods, sessions and memberships are then removed and your profile
  anonymised; the workspaces' business records, including what you wrote in them (such as Heidi
  conversations and feedback you sent), stay with the workspace without your name or address.
- Customers in the EU may switch to another provider under the EU Data Act: the export in section 5
  gives all records and files in common formats, free of charge, at any time.
- We may suspend or end access with immediate effect for a serious breach of these terms, in
  particular of section 8. Otherwise we end the service only with 3 months' notice.

- Each plan includes a monthly budget of AI tokens. When it is used up, the AI features pause until
