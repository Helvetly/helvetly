part of the service, under a contract with Helvetly. Customers are told about a new subprocessor
30 days before it starts (see the DPA, section 6).

Depending on your request, Heidi also sends limited results from workspace records you can access (including business and client data), the contents of a document you ask her to read, current page and available control identifiers, and your chosen reply style.

Not subprocessors: Revolut (an invoice only contains a link the customer's client may open), Google
Maps (the platform only opens a link in a new tab when a person clicks a client's address), the
Swiss and EU tax and e-invoicing networks and authorities (the customer exports and submits XML
itself; the platform's Polish KSeF client is not connected to any feature yet), Google Fonts (fonts are bundled at build time and served by Helvetly), and the
customer's own contacts.

Apart from Google Analytics 4 on the website and the platform, listed above, no analytics,
advertising or tracking services are used; the documentation and the client portal use none.
